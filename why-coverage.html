The Hard Truth

The best time to get coverage was yesterday.
The second best time is today.

Every day you wait, coverage becomes harder to qualify for or more expensive. Health changes. Age increases premiums. Waiting costs real money — and sometimes costs everything.

Age affects your rate
Life insurance premiums increase approximately 8–10% for every year you wait. A policy at 55 costs significantly less than the same policy at 62.
Health changes lock you out
A new diagnosis can make you uninsurable for many plans. Applying while you're healthy gives you the most options at the best prices.
Medicare windows are strict
Miss your enrollment window and you face lifetime premium penalties. There are specific dates you must know before you turn 65.
Your family can't wait
Coverage only works if it's in place before something happens. The people who needed it most are the ones who waited.
