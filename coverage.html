02
Term Life Insurance

Protect your family
affordably

Term life is the most straightforward way to protect your family if something happens to you. You choose a coverage amount and term length. If you pass during that term, your beneficiaries receive a tax-free lump sum. Simple, powerful protection.

What's included
  • 10, 20, and 30-year term options
  • Coverage from $50,000 to $2 million or more
  • Level premiums — your rate is locked in and never increases
  • No-exam options for qualifying applicants
  • Same-day approval available with select carriers
$15+
Starting per month for healthy adults. A 55-year-old male can get $250,000 in 20-year term coverage for approximately $28/month.

03
Final Expense Insurance

Give your loved ones
a gift of peace

The average funeral costs $8,000–$12,000. Add medical bills and outstanding debts, and the burden on your family can be significant. Final expense insurance ensures they never have to worry about that. It's one of the most compassionate gifts you can leave behind.

Plan features
  • $5,000 to $35,000 in coverage
  • Whole life insurance — never expires as long as premiums are paid
  • No medical exam required
  • Guaranteed acceptance options available for any health situation
  • Benefits paid directly to your named beneficiary — not through probate
$30–50
Typical monthly cost. A 65-year-old woman can get $15,000 in coverage for approximately $38/month.

04
Supplemental Coverage

Fill the gaps
before they cost you

Even the best health insurance or Medicare plan leaves gaps — deductibles, co-pays, lost income during recovery. Supplemental coverage pays cash directly to you when you need it most, so a health event doesn't become a financial catastrophe.

Coverage types
  • Hospital Indemnity — daily cash benefit for hospital stays
  • Critical Illness — lump sum payment for cancer, heart attack, or stroke
  • Accident Coverage — cash for injuries, ER visits, and recovery costs
  • Short-Term Income Replacement — when illness keeps you from working
Varies
Many plans start at $20–$50/month depending on coverage type and benefit amounts selected.
