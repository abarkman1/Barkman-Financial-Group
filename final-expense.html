Plain English Explanation

What is final expense
insurance, exactly?

Final expense insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed specifically to cover the costs your family will face when you pass — funeral expenses, burial, cremation, medical bills, and any lingering debts.

Unlike term insurance, which expires — this coverage is permanent. It never runs out. As long as you continue making payments, your family is protected for life.

And unlike traditional life insurance, there is no medical exam. We do a brief health review over the phone and match you with the best carrier and rate available for your situation.
