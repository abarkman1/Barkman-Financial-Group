Who We Are

An independent brokerage that answers to you

Barkman Financial Group is an independent insurance brokerage based in Phoenix, Arizona. We are not tied to any single carrier, quota, or product line. We shop across our network of trusted carrier partners and find what genuinely fits — even when that means a less expensive option.

We specialize in Medicare, life insurance, annuities, final expense, and supplemental coverage for families and retirees across more than 20 states. These are significant decisions. We approach each one with the seriousness they deserve.

Our clients are not policy numbers. We know their names, their families, and their goals — and we stay connected long after enrollment.

From the Founder

I did not build Barkman Financial Group to have a company. I built it because I spent years as an EMT, as a caregiver, as a university counselor — sitting with people at the hardest moments of their lives. I have held the hands of people who had nothing. I have watched families scramble to bury someone they loved because nobody planned ahead.

That is why this company exists. Not to sell policies. To protect families. Those are not the same thing.

If a policy is not right for someone, we do not sell it. If it comes down to a commission or an elderly person being able to make ends meet, there is no decision to make. We build this business on what happens after the policy is sold — on whether the people we worked with are genuinely better protected. That is the standard.
