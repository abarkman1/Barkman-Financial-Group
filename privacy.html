1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit a form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, and coverage interests. We collect only what is necessary to help you understand your options.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide insurance guidance and respond to your inquiry. We use it to contact you, connect you with a licensed advisor, and send information about coverage options you have requested.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Information

Barkman Financial Group does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is used only to serve you.

When we submit an application on your behalf, your information is shared with the relevant carrier solely for the purpose of obtaining a quote or completing enrollment.

We use Formspree to process form submissions and Google Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic. Neither is authorized to use your personal information for purposes beyond service delivery.
