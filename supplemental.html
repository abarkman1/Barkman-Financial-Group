What We Offer

Six types of coverage that fill
the real gaps in your plan

🏥Hospital Indemnity

Hospital Indemnity

Cash paid directly to you per day you're hospitalized — to cover deductibles, copays, meals, transportation, or anything else you need. Works with any primary insurance plan.

⚠️Critical Illness

Critical Illness

A lump-sum cash benefit paid upon diagnosis of a covered critical illness — heart attack, stroke, cancer, and others. Use the money however your family needs it.

🦺Accident Coverage

Accident Insurance

Cash benefits for covered accidents — ER visits, broken bones, dislocations, surgeries, and more. Especially valuable for active individuals and families with children.

🦷Dental Insurance

Dental Coverage

Preventive, basic, and major dental coverage — cleanings, fillings, crowns, dentures, and more. Coverage often excluded from Medicare and employer plans. Available as standalone.

👁️Vision Insurance

Vision Coverage

Annual eye exams, prescription glasses, and contact lens coverage. Often bundled with dental for a comprehensive standalone supplemental package.

👂Hearing Coverage

Hearing Aids

Hearing exams and hearing aid coverage — often missing from Medicare and employer plans. Over 48 million Americans have hearing loss. Coverage helps manage a significant expense.
