Plain English

What term life is
and how it works

Term life insurance provides a guaranteed death benefit to your named beneficiaries if you pass away during the coverage period. You choose the term length and coverage amount. Your premium is fixed for the entire term.

It's the most cost-effective way to protect your family's financial future. A healthy 45-year-old can get $500,000 in coverage for about the same monthly cost as a streaming subscription.

When the term ends, you have options: renew at current rates, convert to permanent coverage, or simply let it lapse if protection is no longer needed.

Coverage Options

Three ways to structure your protection

Standard
Level Term

The most straightforward option. Your premium and death benefit remain constant for the entire term. Clean, predictable, no surprises.

  • Fixed premium for 10, 20, or 30 years
  • Death benefit never decreases
  • Most affordable term option
  • Coverage available for qualified applicants
Premium Option
Return of Premium

All your premiums returned to you if you outlive the term. Pay for protection and get your money back either way.

  • 100% of premiums returned if you outlive the term
  • Same death benefit as level term
  • Higher monthly premium than level term
  • Effective forced savings component
Flexible
Convertible Term

Start with affordable term coverage. Convert to permanent whole life or universal life later — without medical underwriting.

  • Convert without new health exam
  • Lock in insurability at current health
  • Flexible if needs change over time
  • Conversion options vary by carrier
