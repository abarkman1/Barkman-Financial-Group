Base W-2 employment with a commission split on top. You are an employee building a career — not a contractor waiting on checks. We pay more than other agencies because stable, focused agents produce better outcomes for clients and for the company.

Inbound & Hot Outbound

Leads are provided. Inbound clients who have already requested information, and hot outbound leads where interest has been confirmed. You focus entirely on the conversation — not on finding people to have it with.
